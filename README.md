# Create: Wire Rider

Ride high-voltage transmission lines and cables using any Create wrench like a zipline.

Compatible with **Create: Electro Energetics** and **Create: Power Grid** on Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge).

---

### Features

- **Zipline with Any Wrench**: Right click any overhead wire while aiming at it with a Create wrench (or any tagged wrench) to hook on.
- **Mid-air Catch**: Hold right click while falling and aiming near a wire to grab on mid-air.
- **Smooth Physics**: Gravity accelerates you down slopes, momentum carries you uphill, and inertia is preserved with critically damped tracking.
- **Continuous Lines**: Slide across poles onto connected wires automatically. Even works when transitioning between Power Grid and Electro Energetics wires connected to the same pole.
- **Lane Switching**: Tap A or D while riding to smoothly hop over to parallel adjacent cables.
- **Leap & Drop**:
  - Press **Space** to catapult forward in your direction of travel with a momentum boost.
  - Press **Shift** for a controlled drop straight down.
- **Electric Hazards (Live Wires)**:
  - Insulated wires are 100% safe.
  - Bare wires carrying voltage periodically generate electrical discharges that trigger HUD reaction prompts.
  - Hit **Space**, **Left Click**, or **Right Click** matching the prompt.
  - Failing, hitting the wrong button, or trying to bail shocks you with voltage-scaled damage and knocks you off.

---

### Controls

- **Right Click** (aiming at wire with wrench): Hook onto wire.
- **W / S**: Control forward/backward momentum along the wire.
- **A / D**: Switch lanes to adjacent parallel wire.
- **Space**: Leap off the line in direction of travel.
- **Shift**: Controlled drop straight down.
- **Space / LMB / RMB**: Electric hazard quick-time events on HUD.

---

### Requirements

- **Minecraft**: 1.21.1
- **NeoForge**: 21.1.84+
- **Create**: 6.0.0+
- **At least one wire mod**:
  - [Create: Power Grid](https://modrinth.com/mod/powergrid)
  - [Create: Electro Energetics](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-electroenergetics)

Both wire mods are optional dependencies. If only one is installed, the mod runs without crashes or missing class errors. If both are installed, both wire types work simultaneously.
